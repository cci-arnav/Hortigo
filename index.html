The Gap Today

The Current Reality of Polyhouse Farming

Premium polyhouse produce passes through multiple intermediaries before reaching consumers. At each stage, value is lost, quality information disappears, and farmers receive only a fraction of the final price.

Farmer receives a fraction of final priceValue & transparency lost at each handoff

  • Farmers rely heavily on intermediaries for market access

  • Premium produce often receives ordinary commodity pricing

  • Buyers struggle to identify reliable and verified suppliers

  • Consumers cannot easily confirm food quality or origin

  • Transparent, documented supply chains are limited

  • Polyhouse farmers have very little digital visibility

Without a verified layer

Premium polyhouse produce is indistinguishable from conventional crops in the market.

What the Passport Captures

Farm Identity

Farm name, location, polyhouse specifications and farmer profile.

Crop Details

Crop variety, plantation date, harvest date, irrigation method and water source.

Inputs & Handling

Fertilizer information, storage facility and transportation records.

Quality & Trust

Certifications, quality test results and consumer ratings.

One QR code is envisioned to unlock the complete story of a crop — from cultivation to delivery. This is a proposed platform capability on the HortiGo roadmap, not a claim of an already-operational system.

Trust & Verification

Trust Is Earned Through Transparency, Not Claims.

Every layer of verification adds another dimension of reliability — from confirming who a farmer is, to documenting how a crop was grown, handled and delivered, to capturing feedback from the buyers and consumers who received it.

Each layer stacks on the one below it, so trust in the platform is built cumulatively, not asserted all at once.
